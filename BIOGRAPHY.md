Known through his alter ego The Golden Jester, TranscendedKev (T.K. – Kevin Chacon) is a contemporary fine artist forging a new frontier in visual culture.

As the creator of the Artworks Formula Seal: Bull‑Bear‑Koi™ — hailed as the most powerful art seal to ever exist — he pioneers Next‑World‑Class original artworks and assets merging:

- Revolutionary aesthetics
- Narrative innovation
- Transformative creative frameworks

His work challenges conventions, reframes value, and positions art as a multidimensional force spanning the past, present, and future.

Origins & Early Life

Born in 1996, Kevin was raised with strong morals, respect, love, coexistence, and the Golden Rule — values that shaped his perspective on life and creativity.

Naturally introverted, he found connection through:
- Drawing
- Sculpting
- Imagination
- Visual storytelling

During high school, a life‑changing battle with Bell’s Palsy transformed his inner world and deepened his connection to artistic expression. While others socialized, he created — discovering not only skill, but what he believes to be a true gift and calling.

Artistic Vision

TranscendedKev views art as a force beyond aesthetics or commerce — connected to:
- Emotion

His work centers on:
- Originality
